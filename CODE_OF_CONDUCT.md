# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in
the Toolbox community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind, patient and welcoming — especially to first-time contributors
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use Toolbox

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and
may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — issues, pull requests,
discussions — and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer,
[@Ale-Newport](https://github.com/Ale-Newport), through a GitHub direct channel
or a private security advisory if nothing else fits. All reports are reviewed
promptly and confidentially.

## Enforcement

1. **Correction** — a private, written warning explaining why the behaviour was
   inappropriate.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour.
3. **Temporary ban** — no interaction with the community for a set period.
4. **Permanent ban** — for a pattern of violations or harassment.
